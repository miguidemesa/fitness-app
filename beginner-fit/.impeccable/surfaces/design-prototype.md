Scope: Beginner Fit mobile app — full core flow (onboarding, health check, plan, today, logging, weekly changes, progress). Mode: Operate. Delivered as a Design canvas: wireframes, system sheet, interactive prototype (390x844).
Audience/job: nervous gym novice gets a plan and follows it without help; logs how each exercise felt; sees Coach Juno explain changes.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The app is the community-centre notice board where "Beginners welcome!" classes get pinned — local, handmade, unintimidating. Refuses the dark, metric-heavy performance dashboard.
OWN-WORLD: Cork ground framing bright copy-paper flyers (lemon, pink, green, orange, sky, white) in photocopy-black ink; coloured pushpins; Juno speaks on handwritten sticky notes (Kalam); flyer headlines in Bricolage Grotesque condensed-heavy; body in Atkinson Hyperlegible. States carry label + pattern (dotted easy, solid right, hatched hard, zigzag pain). Raises: numbered margin column with one pin on the current exercise (from origami); changed rows re-pin and highlight in place (from split-flap); never colour alone (from cyclorama).
STORY: Juno asks plain questions, checks health honestly, pins this week's flyers; you tear off a tab when done; next week Juno re-pins what changed and says why.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Today = cork board, one big flyer "Day 1 · 25 min" with numbered exercises, pin on the current one, tear-off tabs row at its foot; Juno sticky note top-right; primary action "Start with #1" as the flyer's tab.
FORM: community notice board, grounded list position 7, seed 842a0068.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
